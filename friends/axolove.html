Axolove

Axolove was mostly known by his Hungarian SCP Foundation videos, reading SCP articles and making SCP-themed videos (including some let's-plays, Confinement series translation, etc).

First met on 14.01.2018 in Skype during the beginning of SCP - Isolation development, Axolove was the first one to respond to GreatCorn when he posted that the game was in a need of voice actors (second supporter of the game). He was the voice of 2 MTF operatives and Dr. Maynard. His contributions also included, but weren't limited to: art, ideas, inspiration.

GreatCorn once appeared in his animation titled "Ajándék lónak ne nézd a fogát!" (Don't look a gift horse in the mouth!) as a cameo, also a possible appearance was in his "A szám nélküli szoba" (The room with no number).
