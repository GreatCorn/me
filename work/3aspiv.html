DATE: 14.08.2026

DESCRIPTION: Ethnic Ukrainian folk music mixed and mashed into IDM, DnB and various crazy crap. The structure vaguely follows that of a traditional Ukrainian wedding; it isn't very focused, though, perhaps, reinforced by the interlude tracks.
Thanks to pidshkonka for inspiring me to mess around in MuLab again.
Wasn't intended to be a commercial album because of the material; if you have a problem with it then let me know.
The album uses various samples from the "Весілля" album by БЕРВИ (I guess, site long gone so not much info) and other folklore expeditions, probably mostly to Chornobyl Raion villages.
